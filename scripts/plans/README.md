# Zone plan georeferencing (offline)

Turns the official zone plan images (ViewZone.aspx Deep Zoom tiles) into `public/plans/<zid>.{webp,json}` + `index.json`.
Needs macOS (Apple Vision OCR via `swiftc`), Python 3 with `opencv-python-headless` and `numpy`, and a work directory `W`.

```bash
cd W
npx tsx /path/to/repo/scripts/plans/zone_plots.mts zone_plots.json   # plots per official zone from public/dev-snapshot (run from the repo root)
python3 -c "import json; m=json.load(open('public/dev-snapshot/meta.json')); json.dump({z['zid']:{'n':z.get('n'),'city':z['city'],'name':z['name'],'pid':z['pid'],'cityId':z['cityId']} for z in m['official']['zones']}, open('zone_counts.json','w'), ensure_ascii=False)"
swiftc -O -o tools/ocr_files tools/ocr_files.swift
python3 fetch_plans.py plans zones.tsv --all     # zones.tsv: zid, pid, cityId, city, name, flag, sectors (tab-separated)
zsh tools/batch_extract.sh                       # polygons + OCR per plan → plans/zone_<zid>.plots.json
zsh tools/pipeline.sh                            # register (zones with coordinates) → fill digits → export → index
```

Zones without coordinates: `chain.py` (same drawing as a registered neighbour), `match_roads.py` (OpenStreetMap roads from `osm_roads.py`),
`match_sat.py` (satellite edge correlation, only in a tight window around a known point), `georef_master.py` + `pins.py` (city master
plans with a satellite background → project pins), `control.py` (hand control points). Always check with `verify.py` before exporting.
Copy `export/*.webp`, `export/*.json` and `export/index.json` to `public/plans/`.

## Registrations and later tools (2026-11-10)

`reg/<zid>.json` is the registration of every installed zone (affine plan px → Web-Mercator metres; `scale_m_per_px` is
Mercator metres, ground = × cos(lat)). Keep them: several are hand-adopted and cannot be regenerated by `register.py`
(never run `pipeline.sh`'s register step over zones whose `how` is not `ocr`/`icp`; it would also undo the 6-parameter
fits of 3600/3603 and the re-placements of 3792, 4804, 4808). 3611 carries a manual shift, see its `note_shift`.

Screening: median(polygon ground area / official m²) per zone should be 0.82–1.14. Every zone outside that range was
misplaced or mis-scaled except 3779 (plots drawn larger than listed) and 3609 (undetermined). Check it after any new fit.

`contrib/` holds the tools written while finishing the zones (not all polished; paths may need adjusting):
- `F/road_match.py` — correlate a plan's road mask with graded roads in Esri imagery over scale/rotation/translation
  (the method that fixed West 28, Knowledge Village, Akhmim 16); `F/regroup.py` moves a chained group; `F/ov.py` lines overlay.
- `C/` — low-resolution plans (2048 px): whole-plan upscaled tile OCR with voting, re-segmentation of merged plots, resolver
  against the official number list. `C/export_plan.py` is the exporter to use (per-polygon `plot_id` is authoritative).
- `D/` — Arabic-Indic digit OCR (`ocr_files_ar.swift`), coordinate ↔ polygon matching (`identify.py`), polygon repairs.
- `A/` — 1600 px plans: marker-watershed plot extraction (`seg3.py`), slide georeferencing by road-edge correlation.
- `B/` — `extract_small.py` (10 px plots), `refine_osm.py` (chamfer refinement against OSM), `getosm.py` (OSM API fallback).
- `E/bg_match.py` (plan's own satellite background vs imagery), `G/recorner.py` (re-place without re-export), `H/diag.py`, `H/pin_reg.py`.

Not placed: 15 May 3581 (uncertain candidate), Badr 4803 plot numbers (plan too coarse).

## Earlier stages (`past/`, `reg-past/`, `public/plans/past/`)

Zones of offerings that are no longer open (a red ball on the city page) still publish their plan drawings but no plot
list. The maps draw them greyed under the current stage (`src/lib/past-plans.ts`, `addPastPlans` in
`src/components/plan-layer.ts`): images only, no shapes, no status. They live apart from the current plans so that the
sync (which takes plot positions from `public/plans/*.json`) never reads them.

```bash
npx tsx scripts/tools/list-past-zones.mts --city 1 --out W/zones.tsv        # zid, pid, cityId, city, name, flag, sectors, stage, project
python3 past/fetch_past.py W/plans W/zones.tsv                              # one zone at a time; rerun until nothing fails
curl --data-urlencode 'data=[out:json];(way["highway"~"^(motorway|trunk|primary|secondary|tertiary|unclassified|residential|living_street|.*_link)$"](S,W,N,E););out geom;' https://overpass-api.de/api/interpreter -o W/osm/city.json
python3 past/osm_city.py W/osm/city.json W/osm/city10 S,W,N,E 10            # coarse road raster for the search
python3 past/gmap_city.py W/g/tiles W/g/city16 S,W,N,E 16                 # road raster from Google's road map (labels off)
python3 past/batch_g.py W W/zones.tsv W/g/city16 --jobs 5                   # every zone: whole-city search, check, refine → W/place/zg_<zid>.json + preview
python3 past/batch_g.py W W/zones.tsv W/g/city16 --jobs 5 --seeded          # unclear zones again, around their project's clear ones
python3 past/adopt_g.py W W/zones.tsv W/g/city16 reg-past --accept zid,zid  # registrations for the trusted placements
python3 past/export_past.py W W/zones.tsv reg-past ../../public/plans/past
```

- `place_g.py` (run by `batch_g.py`) is the matcher: streets are the gaps between blocks of outlined plots, so the
  drawing's colours don't matter; the plots' size on the drawing gives the scale; the street pattern is correlated with
  Google's road raster over scale, rotation and position. Google's map has the streets of the new districts that
  OpenStreetMap lacks. A placement is clear when its score is ≥ 0.4 and the best place scores ≥ 1.15 × the next best.
- Always look at the previews (`W/place/zg_<zid>.jpg`: the roads in red on the plan) of zones that score well without
  being clear, and pass the ones that fit to `adopt_g.py --accept`. Of 27 such New Cairo zones, 8 fitted.
- Some projects publish one sheet of the whole district per zone, with only the highlight changing: `adopt_g.py` gives
  such zones the placement of a trusted zone on the same sheet (refined on their own streets when possible).
- The older route (`place.py`, `refine_axis.py`, `overlay.py`, `chain_slide.py`, `compose.py`: project overview slide
  against OpenStreetMap roads, zones located inside the slide) is kept for projects whose inner streets are on no map
  yet; only Cairo Walk was placed that way.
- `export_past.py` keeps only the zone's own drawing (its plots are vivid, the neighbours veiled) and makes the rest
  transparent. `tests/plans-past-data.test.ts` checks the exported index and files.
- The official site throttles a busy address: three copies of `fetch_plans.py` at once made every request time out.
  `fetch_past.py` runs one zone at a time and takes roughly two minutes per zone.

Placed so far (2026-11-10): New Cairo, 146 of 170 earlier zones (plus Cairo Walk's 5 from the OpenStreetMap route).
Second-round tools for what the matcher missed: `street_mask.py` (street masks from a sheet's grey road fill, for
district sheets whose plots are too fine to outline; used for the Beit El Watan 3rd and 4th district sheets),
`via_sheet.py` (a detail drawing registered by image features on an already placed sheet or neighbour: north Rehab's
13 drawings are crops of one master drawing; zone 82 via zone 83), and placement through the project overview slide
for the Narges extension (165, 166, 172, 175). North Rehab is the least certain: Google's newer streets there differ
from the old drawing in places.
Zone 1475 (6th district M) was removed on review: it had been given a sheet-mate's position that its own streets did
not confirm. New Obour (2026-11-10): 14 of 27 zones (projects 70 and 80 publish the same six zones, so they are drawn
twice in the same place); 358–360 rejected for the same reason as 1475.
Not placed: 8th-stage Narges extension (250–256), Amal triangle (288–292), and the small 8th/9th/10th-stage pockets
(315, 316, 350–353, 429–433, 1509): no unique match, and Google has few streets at several of them.
